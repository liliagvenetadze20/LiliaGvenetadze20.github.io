What drives life expectancy across income groups?

Business question
How do wealth, health spending, education spending and infant mortality relate to life expectancy in countries at different income levels?
Data and cleaning
Five World Bank indicators for 21 countries in four income groups, 2000 to 2020. I reshaped the files, merged them by country and year, filled gaps by interpolation within each country, capped outliers with the IQR rule and ran a data quality report before and after.
Analysis
New features: log GDP, a Health Burden Index and Human Capital Investment. Nine charts covering distributions, correlations and trends by income group. I compared Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest models by R² and MSE, then grouped countries with K-Means clustering.

TableauGroup project

Formula 1: reliability, drivers and constructors

Business question
Which constructors and countries see the most races ended by retirement, and how have top drivers and teams performed across seasons?
Data and calculations
The Formula 1 World Championship dataset (races, results, drivers, constructors, circuits). The workbook classifies every result as finished or did not finish, then calculates DNF rate per constructor, average DNFs per race by country and constructor points per year using fixed LOD expressions. Country names were standardised and missing positions filled.
Views
Eleven worksheets in a Tableau story, including race and DNF maps, top 10 drivers and constructors, grid versus finishing position, KPI sheets and a constructor forecast.
  • Tableau
  • LOD expressions
  • Maps
  • KPIs
